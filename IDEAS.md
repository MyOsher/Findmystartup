# Findmystartup — Idea Brainstorm

*Goal: build a company that solves a real problem, is achievable to launch, and could be acquired later.*

## What makes a startup sellable (acquirable)

1. **Recurring revenue (SaaS)** — buyers pay for predictable income, not one-off projects.
2. **A niche you dominate** — better to own 100% of a small market than 0.1% of a huge one.
3. **Proprietary data or workflow lock-in** — something an acquirer can't easily rebuild.
4. **Low founder-dependence** — the product works without you personally doing the work.
5. **A clear list of potential buyers** — you should be able to name 3–5 companies that would want it before you start.

## Your unfair advantage

Real estate domain expertise (Israeli market), hands-on experience with:
- Meta/Instagram lead-generation campaigns funneled to WhatsApp
- Property valuation reports built from real transaction data (nadlan.gov.il) + active listings
- Back-office automation (receipts/invoices from Gmail to Drive)

Ideas that use this advantage beat generic ideas every time.

## Idea 1 (RECOMMENDED): "Win the Listing" — valuation & pitch tool for Israeli real estate agents

**Problem:** When an Israeli agent (מתווך) competes for an exclusive listing (בלעדיות),
they need a professional, data-backed valuation presentation for the seller. Today most
agents improvise: screenshots from Yad2/Madlan, a WhatsApp message, maybe a hand-made PDF.
It takes hours and looks unprofessional — and the agent with the best pitch wins the listing.

**Product:** Agent enters an address → the tool pulls real closed transactions on that
street + active competing listings → produces a branded, polished valuation page (web + PDF)
with the agent's logo, in minutes. Sold as a monthly subscription (₪99–299/month per agent).

**Why you:** You already do this manually for your own clients. You are customer zero.
**Why it's sellable:** Clear acquirers — Madlan, Yad2, CRM providers for agents (e.g. Webtiv,
Blentz), or an international proptech entering Israel. Recurring revenue, sticky workflow.
**MVP scope:** One address → one report. No accounts, no billing — validate with 10 agents first.

## Idea 2: WhatsApp lead-qualification assistant for local-business ad campaigns

**Problem:** Small businesses (agents, clinics, contractors) run Meta ads that dump leads
into WhatsApp — then lose most of them because nobody answers fast, qualifies, or follows up.

**Product:** An AI assistant that answers new WhatsApp leads instantly (Hebrew-first),
asks qualifying questions, books appointments, and pushes a clean summary to the owner.

**Why you:** You run exactly this funnel today and know where leads die.
**Risk:** Crowded space globally; Hebrew-first + vertical focus (real estate) is the edge.

## Idea 3: Automated "deal file" compliance for Israeli brokers

**Problem:** Every Israeli brokerage deal requires paperwork (הזמנת שירותי תיווך, disclosure
forms, signatures) — agents manage it in WhatsApp photos and paper. Lost forms = lost commission lawsuits.

**Product:** Digital deal-file: send client a link, they sign the broker agreement digitally,
everything archived per-deal. Sold per-agent subscription.

**Why you:** You live the paperwork. **Risk:** Requires care around legal validity of digital signatures.

## Pricing model (decided: hybrid, usage-first)

Pure subscription is a hard first sale to Israeli agents ("another standing order");
pure pay-per-use hurts the future sale price (acquirers pay high multiples for
recurring revenue, low multiples for lumpy usage revenue). So: usage-based as the
entry door, subscription as the destination.

| Tier | Price | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| First report | Free | Zero-friction trial before a real listing pitch |
| Single report | ₪49 | Pay only when competing for an exclusive — value moment pricing |
| 5-report pack | ₪199 | Light discount, prepaid commitment |
| Monthly subscription | ₪149/month | Up to 10 reports + full branding (logo, colors) + auto-refresh of reports when a new deal closes on the street |

The math does the conversion: an active agent pitching 3–4 exclusives/month pays
₪150–200 per-use — the subscription becomes the obvious choice. Usage pricing
acquires customers; subscription builds the ARR that makes the company sellable.

## Recommended path

1. Validate Idea 1 by using it on your own next 3 listings + showing it to 10 agents you know.
2. If 3+ say "I'd pay for this," build the MVP (address in → branded report out).
3. Charge from day one — ₪49/report. Paying users are the only real validation.
4. Revisit sellability after ~50 paying agents / meaningful subscription ARR:
   that's when acquirers start to care.
